Ali ibn Abi Talib

The Prophet's cousin, raised in his house from the age of five; the first youth to believe; the husband of Fatimah and father of Hasan and Husayn; the man who slept in the Prophet's bed on the night of the Hijrah; the champion of Badr, Uhud, the Trench and Khaybar; the Gate of the City of Knowledge; the judge whose rulings amazed the Companions; the fourth caliph; and the fountainhead of the spiritual orders of Islam. "None loves you but a believer, and none hates you but a hypocrite."

Section 1

Life at a glance

Ali said: "I used to follow him as a baby camel follows its mother." He never knew a day of idolatry, and he never knew a day away from the Prophet ﷺ until the Prophet ﷺ was gone.

c. 600 CE · 13 Rajab
Birth
Al-Hakim writes in al-Mustadrak that reports of his birth inside the Ka'bah are mass-transmitted. His mother, Fatimah bint Asad, was so loved by the Prophet ﷺ that he wrapped her in his own shirt at her burial.
c. 605
Raised by the Prophet ﷺ
In a year of famine the Prophet ﷺ took Ali into his home to ease Abu Talib's burden. He grew up under the Prophet's care before revelation began.
610
First of the youth
Aged about ten, he saw the Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah praying, asked what it was, and believed. Ibn Abd al-Barr records the majority view that he was the first male to pray.
622 · 1 AH
The night in the Prophet's bed
He lay under the Prophet's green cloak so the Quraysh would think the Prophet ﷺ still slept. He returned the trusts to their owners and walked to Madinah with swollen feet.
623 · 2 AH
Marriage to Fatimah
His dowry was his armour. The Prophet ﷺ prayed: "O Allah, bless them both, bless their union and bless their offspring."
624 · 2 AH
Badr
Killed al-Walid ibn Utbah in the opening duel; Hasan was born the next year, Husayn the year after.
625 · 3 AH
Uhud
Stood by the Prophet ﷺ when most had fallen back, receiving sixteen wounds. The cry was heard: "There is no youth like Ali, and no sword like Dhu'l-Fiqar."
627 · 5 AH
The Trench
Slew Amr ibn Abd Wudd, the champion of Arabia, whom no one else dared face.
628 · 6 AH
Scribe of Hudaybiyah
Wrote the treaty in the Prophet's presence, and out of love could not erase "Messenger of Allah" with his own hand; the Prophet ﷺ erased it himself.
629 · 7 AH
Khaybar
"I shall give the banner to a man who loves Allah and His Messenger and whom Allah and His Messenger love." The Prophet ﷺ cured his eyes with his blessed saliva.
630 · 8 AH
Conquest of Makkah
Stood on the Prophet's shoulders to throw down the idols from the Ka'bah (Musnad Ahmad; al-Hakim).
631 · 9 AH
Judge of Yemen · Bara'ah
"O Allah, guide his heart and make his tongue firm." Sent to proclaim the opening verses of Surah al-Tawbah at the Hajj.
632 · 10–11 AH
Ghadir Khumm · the Prophet's passing
"Whoever I am his Mawla, Ali is his Mawla." Months later he washed the Prophet's blessed body and stood in his grave.
632–656
The counsellor of three caliphs
Judge, adviser and mufti under Abu Bakr, Umar and Uthman; Umar: "Were it not for Ali, Umar would have perished."
656 · 35 AH
Caliphate
Pledged by the Emigrants and Helpers after the martyrdom of Uthman. Moved the capital to Kufa.
656–658
Jamal, Siffin, Nahrawan
The three conflicts the Prophet ﷺ had foretold; "O Allah, turn the truth with him wherever he turns."
661 · 21 Ramadan 40 AH
Martyrdom
Struck with a poisoned sword by the Kharijite Ibn Muljam at Fajr. His last instruction: "One blow for one blow; do not mutilate." His last words: "By the Lord of the Ka'bah, I have succeeded."
Section 2

The Quran and Ali

Quran 3:61 · the MubahalahMuslim 2404
فَقُلْ تَعَالَوْا نَدْعُ أَبْنَاءَنَا وَأَبْنَاءَكُمْ وَنِسَاءَنَا وَنِسَاءَكُمْ وَأَنفُسَنَا وَأَنفُسَكُمْ

"Say: come, let us call our sons and your sons, our women and your women, ourselves and yourselves." Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas narrates that when this verse came, the Prophet ﷺ called Ali, Fatimah, Hasan and Husayn and said: "O Allah, these are my family." Ali stands for "ourselves": the Quran makes him the self of the Prophet ﷺ.

Quran 33:33Muslim 2424
إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ اللَّهُ لِيُذْهِبَ عَنكُمُ الرِّجْسَ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمْ تَطْهِيرًا

"Allah only wishes to remove all impurity from you, O People of the House, and to purify you thoroughly." Aishah narrates the Hadith of the Cloak: the Prophet ﷺ gathered Ali, Fatimah, Hasan and Husayn beneath his cloak and recited this verse.

Quran 5:55al-Wahidi · al-Razi · al-Khazin · Khaza'in al-Irfan
إِنَّمَا وَلِيُّكُمُ اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا الَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ الزَّكَاةَ وَهُمْ رَاكِعُونَ

"Your protector is only Allah, His Messenger, and the believers who establish prayer and give zakat while bowing." Many commentators record that it came down when Ali gave his ring to a beggar while in ruku'. Khaza'in al-Irfan states this plainly.

Quran 69:12al-Tabari · Ibn Abi Hatim · al-Durr al-Manthur
وَتَعِيَهَا أُذُنٌ وَاعِيَةٌ

"…and that a retaining ear may retain it." The Prophet ﷺ said to Ali: "I asked Allah to make it your ear." Ali said: "I never forgot anything after that."

Quran 42:23al-Razi · al-Zamakhshari · Ibn Hajar al-Haytami
قُل لَّا أَسْأَلُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ أَجْرًا إِلَّا الْمَوَدَّةَ فِي الْقُرْبَىٰ

"Say: I ask of you no reward except love of the near kin." Ibn Abbas: the Companions asked who these near kin were; the Prophet ﷺ said: "Ali, Fatimah and their two sons."

Quran 58:12Tirmidhi 3300
إِذَا نَاجَيْتُمُ الرَّسُولَ فَقَدِّمُوا بَيْنَ يَدَيْ نَجْوَاكُمْ صَدَقَةً

"When you consult the Messenger privately, give charity before your consultation." Ali: "There is a verse no one acted on before me and no one will after me." He gave a dinar, asked the Prophet ﷺ ten questions, and the verse was then lifted.

Quran 76:8al-Zamakhshari · al-Razi · al-Khazin
وَيُطْعِمُونَ الطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا

"They feed, for love of Him, the needy, the orphan and the captive." Narrated about Ali and Fatimah, who fasted three days for the recovery of Hasan and Husayn and gave away their only bread each evening.

Quran 2:207al-Tha'labi · al-Razi · al-Hakim 3/4
وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَن يَشْرِي نَفْسَهُ ابْتِغَاءَ مَرْضَاتِ اللَّهِ

"Among people is he who sells his own self seeking the pleasure of Allah." Recorded about the night of the Hijrah, when Ali slept in the Prophet's bed with death expected at dawn.

Quran 98:7al-Tabari · al-Durr al-Manthur
إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ

"Those who believe and do good: they are the best of creation." Jabir reports the Prophet ﷺ said to Ali when it was revealed: "That is you and your followers on the Day of Rising."

Section 3

What the Prophet ﷺ said about him

Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal said: "No Companion has had as many virtues narrated about him as Ali."

al-Hakim 3/126 · al-Tabarani · Ibn Hajar, al-Suyuti: hasan
أَنَا مَدِينَةُ الْعِلْمِ وَعَلِيٌّ بَابُهَا، فَمَنْ أَرَادَ الْعِلْمَ فَلْيَأْتِ الْبَابَ

"I am the City of Knowledge and Ali is its Gate; whoever wants knowledge, let him come to the Gate." Al-Hakim declared it authentic; Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani examined its chains and ruled it sound. Tirmidhi (3723) has the wording "I am the House of Wisdom and Ali is its gate."

Bukhari 4416 · Muslim 2404
أَنْتَ مِنِّي بِمَنْزِلَةِ هَارُونَ مِنْ مُوسَى إِلَّا أَنَّهُ لَا نَبِيَّ بَعْدِي

"You are to me as Harun was to Musa, except that there is no prophet after me." Said when he left Ali in charge of Madinah for Tabuk.

Tirmidhi 3713 · Ibn Majah 121 · Musnad Ahmad · al-Suyuti: mutawatir
مَنْ كُنْتُ مَوْلَاهُ فَعَلِيٌّ مَوْلَاهُ، اللَّهُمَّ وَالِ مَنْ وَالَاهُ وَعَادِ مَنْ عَادَاهُ

"Whoever I am his Mawla, Ali is his Mawla. O Allah, befriend whoever befriends him, and be an enemy to whoever is his enemy." Declared at Ghadir Khumm on the return from the Farewell Hajj. Umar said to him: "Congratulations, Ibn Abi Talib: you have become the Mawla of every believer."

Muslim 78
لَا يُحِبُّكَ إِلَّا مُؤْمِنٌ وَلَا يُبْغِضُكَ إِلَّا مُنَافِقٌ

"None loves you but a believer, and none hates you but a hypocrite." Ali: "By Him who split the seed and created the soul, the Unlettered Prophet ﷺ promised me this."

Bukhari 3702 · Muslim 2406
لَأُعْطِيَنَّ الرَّايَةَ غَدًا رَجُلًا يُحِبُّ اللهَ وَرَسُولَهُ وَيُحِبُّهُ اللهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

"Tomorrow I shall give the banner to a man who loves Allah and His Messenger and whom Allah and His Messenger love." Umar said: "I never wished for leadership except that day."

Tirmidhi 3716 · Bukhari 4251
عَلِيٌّ مِنِّي وَأَنَا مِنْ عَلِيٍّ

"Ali is from me and I am from Ali." Bukhari's wording: "You are from me and I am from you."

Tirmidhi 3712 · Nasa'i, al-Khasa'is
إِنَّ عَلِيًّا مِنِّي وَأَنَا مِنْهُ، وَهُوَ وَلِيُّ كُلِّ مُؤْمِنٍ بَعْدِي

"Ali is from me and I am from him, and he is the wali of every believer after me."

Tirmidhi 3714
رَحِمَ اللهُ عَلِيًّا، اللَّهُمَّ أَدِرِ الْحَقَّ مَعَهُ حَيْثُ دَارَ

"May Allah have mercy on Ali. O Allah, turn the truth with him wherever he turns."

Abu Dawud 3582 · Ibn Majah 2310 · al-Hakim: sahih
اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ قَلْبَهُ وَثَبِّتْ لِسَانَهُ

"O Allah, guide his heart and make his tongue firm." Prayed when sending him to judge in Yemen. Ali said: "I never doubted in a judgment between two people afterwards."

al-Hakim 3/124 · al-Tabarani
عَلِيٌّ مَعَ الْقُرْآنِ وَالْقُرْآنُ مَعَ عَلِيٍّ، لَنْ يَتَفَرَّقَا حَتَّى يَرِدَا عَلَيَّ الْحَوْضَ

"Ali is with the Quran and the Quran is with Ali; they will not part until they come to me at the Pool."

Bukhari 441 · Muslim 2409
قُمْ أَبَا تُرَابٍ

Finding Ali asleep in the mosque with dust on his back, the Prophet ﷺ wiped it off and said: "Get up, Abu Turab." Sahl ibn Sa'd said: "No name was dearer to Ali than that one."

Muslim 1066 · Bukhari 3611
وَاللهِ مَا كَذَبْتُ وَلَا كُذِبْتُ

The Prophet ﷺ described a Kharijite with a withered arm. After Nahrawan the body could not be found. Ali said: "By Allah, I have not lied nor was I lied to. Search the ditch." They found him, and Ali prostrated in thanks.

Musnad Ahmad · al-Hakim 3/113 · Nasa'i, al-Khasa'is
أَشْقَى النَّاسِ… الَّذِي يَضْرِبُكَ عَلَى هَذِهِ حَتَّى تُخْضَبَ مِنْهَا هَذِهِ

"The most wretched of people… is the one who will strike you here" (his head) "until this" (his beard) "is dyed from it." Ali repeated the prophecy often; it was fulfilled exactly.

Section 4

The Companions on Ali

Umar ibn al-Khattab

أَقْرَؤُنَا أُبَيٌّ وَأَقْضَانَا عَلِيٌّ

"Ubayy is the best reciter among us, and Ali is the best judge among us" (Bukhari 4481). Also: "I seek refuge in Allah from a problem for which Abu al-Hasan is not present," and, after Ali corrected him on the insane woman and the pregnant woman: "Were it not for Ali, Umar would have perished" (al-Isti'ab; al-Bayhaqi). Umar married Ali's daughter Umm Kulthum.

Abu Bakr al-Siddiq

On the day of the pledge, Ali and Abu Bakr met and Ali affirmed: "We know your honour and what Allah has given you; we were not angry with you." Abu Bakr answered: "By Him in whose hand is my soul, the family of the Messenger of Allah is dearer to me than my own family" (Bukhari 3712). Abu Bakr said: "Look at the family of the Messenger of Allah among you and guard them."

Abdullah ibn Abbas

لَقَدْ أُعْطِيَ عَلِيٌّ تِسْعَةَ أَعْشَارِ الْعِلْمِ، وَايْمُ اللهِ لَقَدْ شَارَكَهُمْ فِي الْعُشْرِ الْعَاشِرِ

"Ali was given nine-tenths of knowledge, and by Allah he shared with them in the tenth too." Also: "What I took of the tafsir of the Quran, I took from Ali." (al-Isti'ab; Hilyat al-Awliya)

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud

إِنَّ عَلِيًّا عِنْدَهُ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ الظَّاهِرُ وَالْبَاطِنُ

"The Quran was revealed in seven modes; each has an outward and an inward, and Ali has the outward and the inward of it." Also: "The best judge in Madinah is Ali." (Hilyat al-Awliya 1/65; al-Hakim)

Aishah, Mother of the Believers

أَمَا إِنَّهُ أَعْلَمُ مَنْ بَقِيَ بِالسُّنَّةِ

"He is the most knowledgeable of those who remain about the Sunnah." (al-Isti'ab; al-Riyad al-Nadirah). She said it after the Battle of the Camel, above every difference.

Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, Ata and Hasan al-Basri

Sa'id: "None of the Companions used to say 'Ask me' except Ali." Ata: "I know of no one more knowledgeable than Ali." Hasan al-Basri: "Ali was the divinely learned scholar (rabbani) of this community."

Section 5

The Gate of Knowledge

What set Ali apart among the caliphs was the sheer range of his knowledge: the Quran, the law, the language, the earlier scriptures, the future, and the heart.

The Quran

"Ask me before you lose me. By Allah, there is no verse but I know whether it came down by night or by day, on a plain or a mountain" (Ibn Sa'd; Hilyat al-Awliya). The recitation of Hafs from Asim, read by most of the Muslim world, was taught to Asim by Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, who learned the whole Quran from Ali.

Judgment

The lion pit of Yemen (Musnad Ahmad 573): four men died pulling each other into a pit; Ali apportioned blood-money a quarter, a third, a half and full, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "Ali has judged rightly." The six-month pregnancy: he freed a woman by adding the verses "thirty months" (46:15) and "two full years" (2:233). The insane woman: "the pen is lifted from three" (Abu Dawud 4399). The loaves and the eight dirhams, the egg-white accusation, the three men claiming one child (Abu Dawud 2269): all recorded in Ibn al-Qayyim's al-Turuq al-Hukmiyyah.

Language

He is the founder of Arabic grammar. He gave Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali its first principle: "Speech is noun, verb and particle," and told him "follow this way" (unhu hadha al-nahw), from which the science takes its name.

Eloquence

Al-Jahiz ranked his speeches above all others. Scholars said his words were "below the speech of the Creator and above the speech of the created." His letter to Malik al-Ashtar on governing Egypt is still studied as a charter of just rule.

The earlier scriptures

"Were a cushion set for me, I would judge the people of the Torah by their Torah, the Gospel by their Gospel, the Psalms by their Psalms and the Furqan by their Furqan" (Ibn Asakir; al-Riyad al-Nadirah).

The future

The Prophet ﷺ told him he would fight the pledge-breakers, the unjust and the deviants (al-Hakim 3/139). Before Nahrawan he said: "Not ten of them will escape and not ten of you will be killed"; fewer than ten fled and nine of his men fell (Musnad Ahmad).

The heart

Nearly every spiritual order in Islam traces its chain through him: the Qadiri, Chishti, Suhrawardi, Shadhili and Rifa'i orders, as well as the line of Hasan al-Basri. His counsel to Kumayl: "Knowledge is better than wealth: knowledge guards you, you guard wealth. Wealth decreases with spending, knowledge increases."

The dot beneath the Ba

كُلُّ مَا فِي الْقُرْآنِ فَفِي الْفَاتِحَةِ، وَكُلُّ مَا فِي الْفَاتِحَةِ فَفِي الْبَسْمَلَةِ، وَكُلُّ مَا فِي الْبَسْمَلَةِ فَفِي الْبَاءِ، وَكُلُّ مَا فِي الْبَاءِ فَفِي النُّقْطَةِ الَّتِي تَحْتَ الْبَاءِ

"All that is in the Quran is in the Fatihah; all that is in the Fatihah is in the Bismillah; all in the Bismillah is in the Ba; and all in the Ba is in the dot beneath the Ba." Quoted by al-Alusi (Ruh al-Ma'ani) and Isma'il Haqqi (Ruh al-Bayan); the spiritual tradition adds his words "and I am the dot beneath the Ba." Imam al-Ghazali records in the Ihya: "Had I wished, I could have loaded seventy camels with the commentary of the Fatihah."

Section 6

The caliphate

Five years of trial, in which the Prophet's word that truth would turn with Ali was tested and proven.

The pledge

After Uthman's murder the Companions of Madinah came to him; he refused, then accepted when they insisted: "Then in the mosque, in public, for whoever is pleased." Talhah and Zubayr pledged, and the Emigrants and Helpers after them.

Justice

He removed the governors, distributed the treasury equally, walked the market with a whip to check weights, took his own case against a Jewish man before his own judge and lost, and said: "The weak among you is strong with me until I take his right for him."

The Camel

Aishah, Talhah and Zubayr demanded justice for Uthman's blood. Ali sought reconciliation; agitators started the fighting at night. Afterwards he sent Aishah back to Madinah with honour and an escort of forty women, prayed over the dead of both sides and said: "Both of them (Talhah and Zubayr) are in Paradise."

Siffin

Mu'awiyah withheld his pledge pending punishment of Uthman's killers. After months at Siffin the Quran was raised on lances and arbitration accepted. Ammar ibn Yasir was killed in Ali's army; the Prophet ﷺ had said "the rebellious party will kill Ammar" (Bukhari 2812). Ali said of his opponents: "They are our brothers who have transgressed against us."

Nahrawan

The Kharijites left his army, declared everyone unbelievers and killed the Companion Abdullah ibn Khabbab. Ali sent Ibn Abbas to debate them, brought back thousands, and fought the rest exactly as the Prophet ﷺ had foretold.

Martyrdom

Three Kharijites swore to kill Ali, Mu'awiyah and Amr. Ibn Muljam struck Ali at dawn in the mosque of Kufa on 19 Ramadan 40 AH. He forbade torture of his killer, asked his sons to fear Allah, and passed away on 21 Ramadan, aged 63, the age of the Prophet ﷺ. Hasan was pledged and, six months later, handed the caliphate to Mu'awiyah, fulfilling the Prophet's word that "this son of mine is a sayyid; Allah will reconcile two parties through him" (Bukhari 2704).

Section 7

Character and sayings

"The worth of every person is what he knows well."
Ibn Abd al-Barr, Jami' Bayan al-Ilm.
"Do not look at who spoke; look at what was said."
Ibn Abd al-Barr.
"People are asleep; when they die, they wake."
Ihya Ulum al-Din.
"Let none of you be ashamed, when asked what he does not know, to say: I do not know."
Jami' Bayan al-Ilm; Hilyat al-Awliya.
"The world is a place of passing, not a place of staying."
Hilyat al-Awliya 1/76.
"Two kinds of people will be ruined on my account: a lover who exaggerates and a hater who slanders."
Musnad Ahmad; Hilyat al-Awliya.
"Knowledge calls out for action; if it is answered, good; otherwise it departs."
Hilyat al-Awliya.
"Whoever knows his own self knows his Lord."
Attributed to him in the spiritual tradition.

His austerity

As caliph he ate barley bread from a bag he sealed himself so no one would add butter to it. His cloak was patched; asked why, he said: "The heart is humbled by it, and the believer follows it." He freed a thousand slaves from the earnings of his own hands.

His family

Wife: Fatimah al-Zahra, the Prophet's daughter; after her, Umamah, Umm al-Banin, Khawlah, Layla and others. Sons: Hasan, Husayn, Muhsin, Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyyah, Abbas, Umar, Uthman, Abu Bakr and others. Daughters: Zaynab, Umm Kulthum (wife of Umar), Ruqayyah. He named three of his sons after his three predecessors.

Section 8

Scholars through the ages

Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal

"No Companion of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ has had as many virtues narrated with sound chains as Ali" (al-Hakim 3/107; Fath al-Bari 7/71). His Fada'il al-Sahabah devotes its longest section to Ali.

Imam al-Shafi'i

إِنْ كَانَ رَفْضًا حُبُّ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ ۝ فَلْيَشْهَدِ الثَّقَلَانِ أَنِّي رَافِضِي

"If love of the family of Muhammad is called 'rejection', then let the two worlds witness that I am a rejecter." He also said of Ali: "His enemies hid his virtues from envy and his lovers hid them from fear, yet what lay between filled the east and the west."

Al-Nasa'i

Compiled Khasa'is Amir al-Mu'minin Ali, a whole book of what the Prophet ﷺ said about him, and was killed in Damascus for refusing to narrate virtues of Mu'awiyah that he did not know to be sound.

Al-Ghazali

Records in the Ihya Ali's saying about the seventy camels of Fatihah commentary, and in Ihya's book on the heart quotes him as the model of the scholar who acts on what he knows.

Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani

Examined every chain of "I am the City of Knowledge" and ruled it sound, rejecting the claim that it was fabricated. In Fath al-Bari he writes that Ali's virtues in Bukhari and Muslim alone are more than those of any other Companion save Abu Bakr.

Ibn Hajar al-Haytami

In al-Sawa'iq al-Muhriqah he devotes forty hadiths to Ali's virtues, then insists that these virtues and the caliphate of the three before him are both true and both loved.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan

He calls him "Mawla Ali", "Sher-e-Khuda" and "Mushkil Kusha", and holds that he is the Mawla of every believer as the Prophet ﷺ declared at Ghadir, the Gate of the City of Knowledge, and the head of the Friends of Allah after the Prophets. His own spiritual chain, the Qadiri order, runs from Shaykh Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani through the Imams of Ali's house to Ali himself.

In Fatawa Ridawiyyah he writes that hatred of Ali is hypocrisy by the Prophet's own words, that Ali was on the truth in every conflict of his caliphate as Tirmidhi's hadith declares, and that love of Ali and love of the three caliphs before him are one love, since Ali himself pledged to them, praised them and married into their families. In Kanz al-Iman the verses of Mubahalah, Purification and the Ring in Ruku' are noted in Khaza'in al-Irfan with their connection to him.

His verse from Hada'iq-e-Bakhshish describes the Companions as the stars and the Prophet's household as the ship: the community reaches safety by holding to both.

Al-Dhahabi

"Amir al-Mu'minin, Abu al-Hasan, the Chosen, one of the Ten, the brother of the Prophet ﷺ in the bond of brotherhood, his son-in-law, the champion of Islam, the scholar of the Companions, the judge of the community."
